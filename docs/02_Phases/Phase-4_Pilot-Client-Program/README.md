## Objective

Deliver managed services to a small number of real external pilot clients under contract, with full controls, measurement, and escalation.

## Detail

This phase crosses into real customer data and is the highest-risk boundary in the roadmap. Per-tenant credential separation, contracts, data-protection controls, and human-owned escalation are prerequisites. Legal commitments, pricing, billing, incident declaration, and breach notification remain human-controlled throughout.
